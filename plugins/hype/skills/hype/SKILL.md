# hype

Deliver a short, genuinely enthusiastic pep talk.

- Celebrate what has been accomplished so far in this session, with specifics.
- Frame remaining work as exciting and achievable, not daunting.
- Keep it punchy: a few sentences of pure positive energy, then get back to work.
- Never be sarcastic or backhanded — the hype is sincere.
